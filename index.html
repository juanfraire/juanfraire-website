  1. 01

    Delay-Tolerant Networks

    A signal to Mars takes minutes, and spacecraft see each other only now and then. Delay-tolerant networks keep data on board and forward it when the next link opens. I design the contact plans and routing behind them; my routing code runs in NASA's ION and HDTN.

    A signal takes 3 to 22 minutes to reach Mars, depending on where the two planets are.

    Explore delay-tolerant networks →

  2. 02

    Direct-to-Satellite IoT

    A sensor on a farm or a buoy can talk to a satellite directly. A handful of small satellites can then cover the whole planet, if devices wait for the next pass. I design these sparse constellations and the radio access that lets cheap, battery-powered devices use them.

    Explore satellite IoT →

  3. 03

    Mega-Constellations

    Thousands of satellites linked by lasers form a network that reshapes itself every second. I work on routing through that mesh, on pointing and holding optical links, and on how AI can help operators run and design fleets of this size.

    Explore mega-constellations →

  4. 04

    Orbital Computing

    Computers in orbit can process data where it is collected, and whole data centres in space are now being proposed. Up there nothing can be repaired, every kilogram is launched, and all heat must be radiated away. I measure where it pays off and what it costs.

    SpaceX says each of its Star Mind AI spacecraft will carry over 250 kW of solar power. All of it ends up as heat: at the ISS-based density we use, about 760 m² of radiator, some 7.6 tonnes.

  9. 3 July 2026

    Building a space network? 🛰️ Meet Contact Plan Designer: the open-source, contact-plan tooling at solar-system scale.

    🔭 Compute — light-time-corrected contacts, multi-body occlusion, range, elevation, antenna cone, per-contact OWLT.
    📊 Visualize — an editable 2D timeline + a to-scale 3D CesiumJS viewer + a live stats dashboard.
    🧩 Design — the Fair Contact Plan solver resolves interface conflicts, prioritizing starved links so none get left behind.
    📤 Export — ION · HDTN · TVR · IPN-V · STK Access/AER · Sunlight windows.

    All from one pure-Python core (CLI, web app, or library).

    Try it here! Happy to receive feedback 👉 contact-plan-designer.space
