The Revue Scientifique annuelle (4–6 Nov, Paris) is where Inria takes stock of its core research topics. AI ran through all three days, and space got a lot of attention: a full session on 𝗻𝗼𝗻-𝘁𝗲𝗿𝗿𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗶𝗮𝗹 𝗻𝗲𝘁𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸𝘀, coordinated by Oana I. and Maxime Guillaud.
