🚀 SPACE RG — First Official Meeting at IETF 124 (Montreal)

Today (Nov 6), we (with Jörg Ott, Nishanth Sastry) held the first official meeting of the IRTF Research Group on SPACE at IETF 124 in Montreal, following two earlier side meetings at IETF 122 and 123.

The turnout: ~90 participants onsite + remote, demonstrating the growing momentum around networking research for LEO mega-constellations, lunar infrastructure, and deep-space systems. Our technical agenda covered:

✅ Streaming over LEO — Netflix presented lessons learned in adaptive bitrate and congestion control.
✅ Laser links & scheduling — Amazon Kuiper discussed the challenges of integrating optical downlinks in mega-constellations.
✅ AI-enabled constellations — New tools for simulation, analysis, and testbeds.
✅ Real-world measurements — Live results on end-to-end performance across operational LEO systems.
✅ New drafts — A proposed framework to simplify constellation classification and enumeration.

Beyond our own work, SPACE RG aims to feed and collaborate with other groups across the IETF/IRTF ecosystem — including DTN, TIPTOP, and TVR — to help align architectures, metrics, and protocol evolution 🌍🛰️📡.
