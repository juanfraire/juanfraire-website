Two days at the Irish College in Leuven 🇧🇪, co-located with IEEE WiSEE 2026, for the 13th edition of the Space-Terrestrial Internetworking Workshop 🛰️.

𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗴𝗿𝗮𝗺𝗺𝗲
🔸 4 keynotes: Ed Birrane (Tolero Networks), Marius Feldmann (D3TN), Felix Flentge (ESA), Dr. Alberto Montilla (Spatiam)
🔸 4 invited talks, 10 peer-reviewed papers, 9 posters
🔸 Dedicated MISSION (https://mission-project.eu/) and D3-CONNECT (https://d3-connect.space/) sessions
🔸 A closing panel on where DTN and the Internet meet, with Kanglian Zhao (Nanjing University) joining the keynote speakers
