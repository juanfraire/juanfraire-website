𝗦𝗣𝗔𝗖𝗘 𝗥𝗚 𝗶𝘀 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘁𝗼 𝗘𝗦𝗔/𝗘𝗦𝗢𝗖 🛰️ 𝗝𝗼𝗶𝗻 𝘂𝘀 𝗶𝗻 𝗗𝗮𝗿𝗺𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗱𝘁 𝗼𝗻 𝟭𝟬 𝗡𝗼𝘃𝗲𝗺𝗯𝗲𝗿!
The next interim meeting of the IRTF SPACE RG will take place at ESA's European Space Operations Centre (ESOC) in Darmstadt, Germany, on Tuesday, 10 November 2026.

The idea is to bring the IETF/IRTF closer to the space community.

🗓️ 𝗪𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝘁𝗼 𝗲𝘅𝗽𝗲𝗰𝘁
• A full day of technical talks on space networking
• Updates on SPACE RG drafts
• Interactive group discussions
• Coffee, lunch, and a tour of the ESOC facilities

🎤 𝗖𝗼𝗻𝗳𝗶𝗿𝗺𝗲𝗱 𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗮𝗸𝗲𝗿𝘀 𝘀𝗼 𝗳𝗮𝗿
• Tomaso de Cola (DLR): Earth-to-Moon networking, comparing end-to-end and DTN approaches
• Andreas Schmidt (Saarland University): sustainable space networking
• Maxime Piraux (Aerospacelab): where IETF and industry visions for constellation networks meet

📍 𝗛𝗼𝘄 𝘁𝗼 𝗷𝗼𝗶𝗻
On-site seats are limited, so if you plan to come to Darmstadt, let us know early. We'll send registration and access details to the SPACE RG mailing list. You can also join remotely via Meetecho.

📣 𝗪𝗼𝗿𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗼𝗻 𝘀𝗼𝗺𝗲𝘁𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗿𝗲𝗹𝗲𝘃𝗮𝗻𝘁 𝘁𝗼 𝗦𝗣𝗔𝗖𝗘 𝗥𝗚? The call for talks is still open. Send me or the list a tentative title and a short description.
