🚀 IETF 124 is underway in Montreal!

🛜 At the IRTF Open Meeting, Renata Teixeira from Netflix kicked off the week with a fascinating keynote, sparking great discussions on how low-Earth-orbit (LEO) constellations are reshaping end-to-end data delivery — including streaming and congestion control across space networks.

🎙️ The conversation continues at our upcoming SPACE RG (Systems and Protocol Aspects for Circumstellar Environments) session this Thursday, November 6, from 9:30–11:00 AM (room Laurier).
