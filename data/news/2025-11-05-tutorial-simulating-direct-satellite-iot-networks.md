🎓 A Tutorial on Simulating Direct-to-Satellite IoT Networks with OMNeT++ and FLoRaSat 🚀

Proud to share that my PhD student, Alexander Ylnner Choquenaira Florez (Inria), with the support of Benoit Coeugnet (my PhD at Kinéis), presented our tutorial at the IEEE LatinCom 2025 in Antigua, Guatemala 🇬🇹 (5–7 November).

The session introduced FLoRaSat, our open-source simulator for Direct-to-Satellite IoT networks, and demonstrated its integration with OMNeT++ for large-scale experiments.
