In the context of the MISSION project (https://mission-project.eu/), we had the pleasure of running a workshop at the AI School of Shenzhen Technology University (#SZTU) on March 12th and 13th.

Great discussions on the role of computer science at the intersection of robotics, AI, and space systems, and the opportunities this convergence opens for future research.

A big thank you to Lijun Zhang for the excellent organization and for bringing together such an engaged audience!
