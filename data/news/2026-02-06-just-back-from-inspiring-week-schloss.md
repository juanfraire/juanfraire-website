Just back from an inspiring week at Schloss Dagstuhl – Leibniz-Zentrum für Informatik (LZI), where I had the privilege of participating in the “Connected Space” seminar.

Dagstuhl seminars are indeed truly unique: designed to foster deep technical exchange among leading experts from academia and industry.

Space is rapidly becoming a computing and networking platform, not just a communications infrastructure. The seminar helped crystallize some of the field’s main future research directions.

Many thanks to the organizers, Nitinder Mohan, Nishanth Sastry, and Paulo Milheiro Mendes, for bringing together such an outstanding group.
