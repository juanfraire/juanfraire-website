Our 𝗦𝗣𝗔𝗖𝗘 𝗥𝗚 🛰️ session at IETF 126 in Vienna brought together around 100 participants again. 🚀

We heard invited talks spanning the next waves of 𝗟𝗘𝗢 𝘀𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗹𝗹𝗶𝘁𝗲 𝗱𝗲𝗽𝗹𝗼𝘆𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁𝘀, the 𝗟𝗶𝗺𝗶𝘁𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀 𝗼𝗳 𝗟𝗘𝗢 𝘂𝗽𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗸𝘀, requirements and challenges for 𝗜𝗻𝗱𝘂𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗶𝗮𝗹 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗺𝘂𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗼𝘃𝗲𝗿 𝗡𝗧𝗡, and comparative approaches to 𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗻𝗱𝗮𝗿𝗱𝗶𝘇𝗲𝗱 𝗿𝗼𝘂𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗟𝗘𝗢 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗹𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀.

The group also moved ongoing work forward with updates on:
🧩  a code for describing satellite constellations; and
🔭  a typology of space research infrastructures.

A big thank-you to Dan York, Liz Izhikevich, Florian Zeiger, Lin Han, Maxime Piraux, Nishanth Sastry, and everyone who joined the discussion - in Vienna and remotely.
