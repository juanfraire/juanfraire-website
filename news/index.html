  7. 23 July 2026

    Our SPACE RG 🛰️ session at IETF 126 in Vienna brought together around 100 participants again. 🚀

    We heard invited talks spanning the next waves of LEO satellite deployments, the Limitations of LEO uplinks, requirements and challenges for Industrial communication over NTN, and comparative approaches to Standardized routing for LEO constellations.

    The group also moved ongoing work forward with updates on:
    🧩  a code for describing satellite constellations; and
    🔭 a typology of space research infrastructures.

    A big thank-you to Dan York, Liz Izhikevich, Florian Zeiger, Lin Han, Maxime Piraux, Nishanth Sastry, and everyone who joined the discussion - in Vienna and remotely.

  8. 3 July 2026

    Building a space network? 🛰️ Meet Contact Plan Designer: the open-source, contact-plan tooling at solar-system scale.

    🔭 Compute — light-time-corrected contacts, multi-body occlusion, range, elevation, antenna cone, per-contact OWLT.
    📊 Visualize — an editable 2D timeline + a to-scale 3D CesiumJS viewer + a live stats dashboard.
    🧩 Design — the Fair Contact Plan solver resolves interface conflicts, prioritizing starved links so none get left behind.
    📤 Export — ION · HDTN · TVR · IPN-V · STK Access/AER · Sunlight windows.

    All from one pure-Python core (CLI, web app, or library).

    Try it here! Happy to receive feedback 👉 contact-plan-designer.space

  10. 24 June 2026

    Santiago Martín Henn has just defended his PhD thesis entitled:

    "Models and algorithms for the design and optimization of space missions based on small satellite constellations"

    at the Facultad de Ciencias Exactas Físicas y Naturales of the UNC.

    It was a huge pleasure to supervise Santi, now my friend, through a thesis at the intersection of astrodynamics and computer science, a line of work that spans both papers he led and standout collaborations:

    🛰️ S. M. Henn, J. A. Fraire, and H. Hermanns, "Polygon-Based Algorithms for N-Satellite Constellations Coverage Computing," IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems, vol. 59, no. 5, pp. 7166–7182, 2023.

    📡 G. Maiolini Capez, S. Henn, J. A. Fraire, and R. Garello, "Sparse Satellite Constellation Design for Global and Regional Direct-to-Satellite IoT Services," IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems, vol. 58, no. 5, pp. 3786–3801, 2022.

    🔭 R. Ortigueira, S. Montejo-Sánchez, S. Henn, J. A. Fraire, and S. Céspedes, "Satellite Visibility Prediction for Constrained Devices in Direct-to-Satellite IoT Systems," IEEE Sensors Journal, 2024.

    🌐 J. A. Fraire, S. Henn, G. Stock, R. Ohs, G. Stock, H. Hermanns, et al., "Quantitative Analysis of Segmented Satellite Network Architectures: A Maritime Surveillance Case Study," Computer Networks, vol. 255, art. 110874, 2024.

    Congratulations, Dr. Henn. Proud of you. 🎓
