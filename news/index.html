  9. 3 July 2026

    Building a space network? 🛰️ Meet Contact Plan Designer: the open-source, contact-plan tooling at solar-system scale.

    🔭 Compute — light-time-corrected contacts, multi-body occlusion, range, elevation, antenna cone, per-contact OWLT.
    📊 Visualize — an editable 2D timeline + a to-scale 3D CesiumJS viewer + a live stats dashboard.
    🧩 Design — the Fair Contact Plan solver resolves interface conflicts, prioritizing starved links so none get left behind.
    📤 Export — ION · HDTN · TVR · IPN-V · STK Access/AER · Sunlight windows.

    All from one pure-Python core (CLI, web app, or library).

    Try it here! Happy to receive feedback 👉 contact-plan-designer.space

  20. 6 November 2025

    🚀 SPACE RG — First Official Meeting at IETF 124 (Montreal)

    Today (Nov 6), we (with Jörg Ott, Nishanth Sastry) held the first official meeting of the IRTF Research Group on SPACE at IETF 124 in Montreal, following two earlier side meetings at IETF 122 and 123.

    The turnout: ~90 participants onsite + remote, demonstrating the growing momentum around networking research for LEO mega-constellations, lunar infrastructure, and deep-space systems. Our technical agenda covered:

    ✅ Streaming over LEO — Netflix presented lessons learned in adaptive bitrate and congestion control.
    ✅ Laser links & scheduling — Amazon Kuiper discussed the challenges of integrating optical downlinks in mega-constellations.
    ✅ AI-enabled constellations — New tools for simulation, analysis, and testbeds.
    ✅ Real-world measurements — Live results on end-to-end performance across operational LEO systems.
    ✅ New drafts — A proposed framework to simplify constellation classification and enumeration.

    Beyond our own work, SPACE RG aims to feed and collaborate with other groups across the IETF/IRTF ecosystem — including DTN, TIPTOP, and TVR — to help align architectures, metrics, and protocol evolution 🌍🛰️📡.

    If you’re interested in contributing to the research agenda, join us:

  25. 28 July 2025

    SPACE RG @ IETF Madrid: packed room, strong signal 🚀

    Last week, we ran the second side‑meeting for the (proposed) IRTF SPACE RG — and it was a clear success: ~60 people on‑site + several 10s remote, spanning space agencies, industry, and academia. The key takeaway: interest in space networking at the IETF/IRTF is strong!

    🛰️What SPACE RG is about (in one line):
    Systems and protocol adaptations for aerospace networks — from HAPS and LEO/MEO/GEO to cislunar and beyond — where mobility, partitions, and constrained resources challenge terrestrial assumptions.

    🎯Our initial goals:
    Build an open data repository for aerospace networks, define reproducible evaluation methodologies, establish best practices and reference scenarios, and foster shared measurement infrastructures.
